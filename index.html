FAQ

Questions engineers ask before a demo

Do we need our own hydraulic model?

If you don't have a model, our expert modelling team is able to assist you.

Which model formats do you import?

InfoWorks ICM network imports and EPA SWMM / VO SWMM models, plus GIS layers (Shapefile / GeoJSON) and CCTV / NASSCO PACP inspection exports.

How do you handle pipes without CCTV data?

They are shown grey and unscored, never as "good". OACS is only calculated where PACP-scored inspection data exists.

How do I make the case to council and budget holders?

We help you do it. Our team works with you to present SSCAP to council and the people who approve the budget in plain, non-technical language: what the map shows, what is at risk, and what the decision is. Ask about council support when you book your demo.
